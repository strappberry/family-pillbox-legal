Privacy Policy

Family Pillbox: Med Reminder (Pastillero Familiar) · Strappberry LLC · Effective October 19, 2026

The short version

Family Pillbox does not collect, store or share your personal data. Everything you enter (patient names, medicines, schedules and the doses you mark as taken) stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no servers of ours, no analytics and no advertising SDKs. We cannot see your medicines or any health information.

Data stored on your device

Patients, medicines, schedules, dose history and your settings are saved locally on your phone. Deleting the app deletes this data. Your phone’s own backup (iCloud or Google) may include it, under Apple’s or Google’s terms.

Notifications

Medicine reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. They are not sent through our servers.

Purchases

The optional Plus subscription is processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment details. The store tells the app only whether your subscription is active.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Medical disclaimer

Family Pillbox is a reminder tool. It does not provide medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your doctor or pharmacist.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if we add optional family sharing), we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
